ABOUT ME · ARTISAN MONOGRAPH

A Vintage Sewing Machine, and the Touch of Time

chapter I

The Dusty Sewing Machine in the Corner

The genesis of my craft began on an ordinary moving day back home. Standing in a quiet, shadowed corner of the old family house, I stumbled upon an antique sewing machine resting quietly under years of dust.

The moment I gently wiped away the dust, long-buried childhood flashbacks flooded back: memories of my grandmother and neighborhood aunts pressing the treadles on lazy afternoons. The rhythmic clack of the needle flying through fabric amidst sunlit dust motes formed my earliest perception of tactile warmth.

Tracing the aged cast-iron wheel and deep wooden grain, an unquenchable spark ignited in my fingertips. I knew then that cloth and thread were my true calling. From that day onward, my journey into slow textiles, botanical dyes, and heirloom rag dolls began—unceasing and unstoppable.

“The moment my fingers touched that dusty machine, memory and destiny met, unstoppable and destined.”
chapter II

Natural Botanical Dyes & Living Patina

In my studio atelier, I extract natural pigments directly from seasonal plants: autumn pomegranate rinds yield rich golden tea, walnut hulls brew grounded timber brown, while perilla and red cabbage transform into dreamy dusty mauve and twilight slate blue across varying pH baths.

Rejecting synthetic chemicals, I use traditional mineral mordants so plant pigments anchor gently into raw cotton and linen fibers, creating a breathable, living patina over time.

chapter III

From a Single Rag Doll to a Timeless Brand

From heirloom rag dolls crafted to be cherished across generations, to micro-tailored pet collars and block-printed ruffle bags—this tactile aesthetic is expanding naturally into an enduring lifestyle brand.

Drawing deep inspiration from the Russian VK handmade community and avant-garde global artisans on Instagram, I look forward to partnering with kindred spirits to build an authentic, heartfelt brand.
